Formula Student

Capturing the raw energy and precision of Formula Student engineering and competition

2024 • 10 Images • Motorsport Photography

About This Project

Formula Student captures the intersection of student engineering excellence and competitive motorsport. This collection documents the raw energy, technical innovation, and split-second intensity that defines the Formula Student competition.

From design and manufacturing to track testing and competition, these images explore the sculptural qualities of student-built racing machines and the focused dedication of young engineers and drivers. Each photograph seeks to convey the passion and precision inherent in Formula Student - where academic learning meets real-world engineering challenges.

Shot with both digital and film cameras, this series celebrates the marriage of educational excellence and competitive spirit, reflecting the pursuit of perfection that drives Formula Student teams worldwide.
