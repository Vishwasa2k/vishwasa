About

Photographer & Visual Storyteller

I'm a photographer drawn to the stark interplay of light and shadow, working primarily in black and white to uncover the silent tension in ordinary scenes. My style is minimalist, often inspired by geometry, contrast, and solitude — echoing the visual storytelling of artists like Ralph Gibson.

I shoot both digital and film (with a Nikon F3), and my current focus is on urban environments and transitional spaces — where chaos, symmetry, and mood collide. I'm also an engineer, which explains the obsession with structure and visual balance. When I'm not behind the lens, I'm building production systems or animating motorsport machines in Blender.

My work explores the relationship between space and emotion, finding beauty in the overlooked corners of modern life. Each frame is a meditation on form, texture, and the poetry of everyday moments frozen in time.
