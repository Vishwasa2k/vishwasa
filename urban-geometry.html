Urban Geometry

Exploring architectural patterns and geometric forms in urban environments

2024 • 5 Images • Street Photography

About This Project

Urban Geometry explores the intersection of architectural design and photographic composition. This series captures the stark beauty found in modern cityscapes, where clean lines, geometric patterns, and dramatic shadows create compelling visual narratives.

Shot primarily in black and white using both digital and film techniques, each image in this collection examines how urban environments shape our visual experience. The interplay between light and shadow reveals the hidden geometry that surrounds us daily, transforming ordinary architectural elements into extraordinary photographic subjects.

This ongoing project represents my fascination with the mathematical precision inherent in urban design and the poetry that emerges when these structured forms are captured through the lens of artistic interpretation.
